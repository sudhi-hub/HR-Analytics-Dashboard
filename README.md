📊 HR Analytics Dashboard

📌 Project Overview

The HR Analytics Dashboard is a data visualization project created to analyze employee workforce information and understand employee attrition. It presents key HR metrics through interactive charts, KPI cards, and slicers, making the data easier to understand.

🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze total employees and employee attrition.
* Calculate the overall attrition rate.
* Understand employee distribution by department and gender.
* Explore attrition across job roles and years at the company.
* Visualize monthly income and job satisfaction.
* Use slicers to filter and explore employee data.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* Microsoft Excel / WPS Office
* PivotTables and PivotCharts
* Data Visualization
* Slicers and KPI Cards
* IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition Dataset

📂 Dataset

The project uses the IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance dataset, containing 1,470 employee records and 35 columns.

The dataset includes information such as:

* Department and job role
* Gender and business travel
* Monthly income
* Job satisfaction
* Years at company
* Attrition status

📈 Dashboard Features

KPI Cards

* Total Employees: 1,470
* Employees Left: 237
* Active Employees: 1,233
* Attrition Rate: 16.12%

Visualizations

* Employee Attrition Distribution
* Employee Count by Department
* Attrition by Job Role
* Gender Split
* Monthly Income vs. Years at Company
* Attrition by Tenure
* Job Satisfaction
* Department Attrition Comparison

Interactive Filters

Slicers allow users to explore the dashboard by:

* Gender
* Department
* Business Travel
* Other available employee categories

🔍 Key Insights

* The dataset contains 1,470 employee records.
* A total of 237 employees left the company.
* The overall attrition rate is approximately 16.12%.
* Research & Development has the largest employee count.
* Charts help compare workforce distribution and attrition across different employee categories.

⚙️ How to Use

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Open the Excel workbook in Microsoft Excel or WPS Office.
3. Open the dashboard worksheet.
4. Use the slicers to filter employee data.
5. Explore the KPI cards and charts to understand workforce patterns.

💡 Learning Outcomes

* Data summarization using PivotTables
* Creating PivotCharts
* Designing an interactive dashboard
* Using slicers for dynamic filtering
* Presenting HR metrics through data visualization

🏁 Conclusion

This project demonstrates how employee data can be transformed into a clear and interactive HR dashboard. It helps users explore workforce patterns and understand employee attrition through visual analysis.
